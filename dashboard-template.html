How the cut works

The season runs 15 scoring weeks (Rounds 2–16, May 5 – Aug 11). Golf Genius keeps each team's best 13 of 15 weekly point totals — the two lowest weeks are dropped. The top 7 teams in each division after drops advance to the shootout on Tue, August 18 (true alternate shot, 2 teams eliminated every 2 holes, division winners meet in a 3-hole aggregate final).

Projection method: each team's remaining rounds are assumed at their current weekly average, then the best 13 of the resulting 15 weeks are summed. "If drops applied today" simply removes each team's two worst weeks so far.

2026 League Format & Rules (from the handbook)

Season Schedule

• Apr 28 — Welcome Fun Night (scramble, tee gift; doesn't count)
• May 5 – Aug 11 — 15-week points race (Rounds 2–16), match play
• Aug 18 — Shootout for top 7 teams per division
• Best 13 of 15 weeks count toward the season total (2 drops; rainouts reduce to best 13 of 14, then 13 of 13)

Weekly Scoring — 27 points per match

• A vs A (lower handicaps): 9 pts, 1 per hole (win 1 / tie ½ / loss 0)
• B vs B (higher handicaps): 9 pts, same per-hole scoring
• Team total vs team total: 9 pts, same per-hole scoring

No-shows

• Missing player: opponent plays vs net par (absent side earns 0 for that match); the short-handed team also can't earn team-total points
• Missing team: present team plays vs par; absent team gets 0 for the week

Handicaps

• GHIN handicaps, updated every Monday before league play
• Max playing handicap: 18 strokes for 9 holes
• Subs use their GHIN, otherwise play scratch; sub name + GHIN due 24 hours ahead (Mon 5:30 PM) or they play scratch

Play Rules

• Men play Blue tees, Ladies play Red
• Ball played down; OB or lost ball: re-hit +1 or drop +2
• Everything holed out — no gimmes

Shootout (Aug 18)

• Top 7 per division; original roster only, spots confirmed by Wed 5:30 PM after the final night
• True alternate shot; same ball until lost, no swapping
• 2 teams eliminated every 2 holes: 7 → 5 → 3 → 1 after six holes
• Division winners play a 3-hole aggregate for the overall title; payouts on elimination
